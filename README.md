# Radio+

A free, open-source FM/AM radio app for compatible Android head units.
Station lists, favorites and your own channel logos. English and Finnish.

**[Download the beta APK](https://github.com/edman01/radio-plus-open/releases)** · [Suomeksi](README.fi.md)

![Radio+ head-unit demo](docs/images/radio-plus-head-unit-demo.png)

*Illustrative AI-assisted demo based on an emulator screenshot. Station logos are not included in the app.*

## Before installing

Requires **Android 8.1+** and the compatible stock radio's **FMPlugService**.
Keep the original radio installed and enabled. Radio+ does not replace it or
change your firmware. Compatibility depends on the head unit, not just its
Android version. This is a beta; real-device audio and steering controls need testing.

## Get started

1. Download `RadioPlus-community-beta.apk` from Releases and install it on your head unit.
2. Open **Tuning** to scan for stations or tune manually.
3. Hold a station card to manage favorites, rename it or change its logo.

[Installation and updates](docs/INSTALL.md)

## Add your logos

Hold a station → **Change logo → Add custom logo from device…**.
Choose a PNG or JPEG from USB storage or your device. Use **Remove logo** to remove it.

160×120 images work; larger originals up to a 512-pixel long side can provide more detail.
Use images you have permission to use. [Full logo guide](docs/LOGOS.md).
